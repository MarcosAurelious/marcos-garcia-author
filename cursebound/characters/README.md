# Cursebound fighter art pipeline

The original six cursed creatures retain their approved designs. New challengers are processed **one at a time**, with consistent 8-frame action rows, alpha-safe margins, and animation QA before activation.

## Current staged sprite packs

- **La Llorona v2.8**: 8 × 12 motion/combat atlas (96 frames), 4 × 2 Wailing Flood atlas (8 frames). Both inspected, corrected, and exported with transparent backgrounds. Asset transfer to GitHub pending.
- **Mothman v2.8**: 8 × 12 motion/combat atlas (96 frames), 4 × 2 Omen Dive atlas (8 frames). All 104 cells pass a 15 px alpha-margin test. Asset transfer to GitHub pending.

Assets must be uploaded to their respective directories before the live site is switched to the expanded roster. **These READMEs do not mean the updated artwork is deployed.**

The original six, current v2.6 gameplay, and Arcade link are unchanged.
