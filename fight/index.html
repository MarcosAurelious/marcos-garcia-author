The fight
Best of three rounds, 60 seconds each. Land hits to fill the blue super meter; when it flashes, unleash your super. Land a hit, then press a stronger attack to chain it: punch → kick → heavy → special. Caught in a combo? Combo breaker costs 40% meter. Crouch to duck projectiles; kicks hit low. Every fighter has a passive power (shown under their name). New to the arena? Try Training mode to practice combos on a dummy.

Photosensitivity notice: this game has flashes and screen shake. Turn on Calm mode in the ⚙ menu if you are sensitive to light.
