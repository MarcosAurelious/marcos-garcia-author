Marcus Aurelius'

The Aurelious Quest

A Field Guide to Swords, Eagles & Electric Storms

✦

The Shadow Legion has torn the scrolls from the Meditations and scattered them across three chapters of the world: a graphite Rome, a laboratory of lightning, and the cosmos itself. Run, leap and fight to win them back.

Your adversaries

  • Chapter I: Shadow Knights and Archers, Pegasus drones, the charging Automaton Minotaur, and the Minotaur Engine.
  • Chapter II: Medusa the Artificer, whose gaze turns you to stone, Helios orbs, Tesla coils, and the Dark Commander.
  • Chapter III: Chiron's star arrows, the Solar Chariot, Augustus Colossus, the Neptune Leviathan, Medusa the Technomancer, and the Roman Automaton.

How to play

  • ←→ run
  • Space or ↑ jump
  • X or J swing the sword
  • On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
  • Find all nine scrolls for a lesson from Marcus.
