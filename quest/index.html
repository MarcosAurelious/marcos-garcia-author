The Shadow Legion has torn the scrolls from the Meditations and scattered them across ten chapters of the world: graphite Rome, laboratories of lightning, the cosmos, and the Underworld itself. Run, leap and fight to win them back.

Ten chapters, twelve bosses

  • I. The Sketchbook Frontier · the Minotaur Engine
  • II. The Weird Science Laboratory · the Dark Commander
  • III. The Cosmic Forum · Augustus Colossus
  • IV. The Sundial at Dawn · Medusa the Technomancer
  • V. The Hall of Synchronicity · the Neptune Leviathan
  • VI. The Storm over the Seven Hills · Asterion the Storm Minotaur
  • VII. The Gates of the Underworld · Cerberus
  • VIII. The Kingdom of Pluto · Pluto
  • IX. The Galvanic Forge · the Roman Automaton
  • X. The Edge of Eternity · the Knights' Gauntlet: Templar Crusader, Golden King, Black Knight

How to play

  • ←→ run
  • Space or ↑ jump
  • X or J swing the sword
  • K or C throw a spear. Every 5 foes you defeat drops 3 spears.
  • On phones, use the on-screen buttons.
  • Calm mode (in the ⚙ settings menu) turns off flashing effects and screen shake.
  • Find all thirty scrolls for a lesson from Marcus.
  • Earn XP from foes, scrolls and chapters to evolve Marcus through five stages. His armor changes as he grows: bronze, a Tesla pack and electric sword, an energy shield, and finally support drones.

Photosensitivity notice: this game has flashing lights and screen shake. If you or your child are sensitive to light or have epilepsy, turn on Calm mode, play in a well-lit room, and stop if you feel dizzy or unwell. Take a break every 30 to 45 minutes; the game will remind you.
